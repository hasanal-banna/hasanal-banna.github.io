About

At the Policy Research Institute of Bangladesh I work with Dr. Zaidi Sattar and Dr. Bazlul Haque Khondker on trade and macroeconomic policy, including the National Board of Revenue’s taskforce on tax reform and the Economic Relations Division’s study of alternatives to cash incentives after LDC graduation. I am a member of the Bangladesh–China Joint Research Team on Export Capacity Enhancement and was named PRI Employee of the Year in 2025.

I hold an M.S.S. (2024) and a B.S.S. (2023) in Economics from the University of Dhaka.
