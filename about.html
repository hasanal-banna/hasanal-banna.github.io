ProfileAbout

Hasan Al Banna is a Senior Research Associate at the Policy Research Institute of Bangladesh in Dhaka, Bangladesh, where he works on trade, taxation and macroeconomic policy. His work combines qualitative and quantitative analysis, policy writing and project coordination for public and private-sector clients.

At the Policy Research Institute of Bangladesh, he has progressed from Research Intern to Research Associate and Senior Research Associate. He was named the Policy Research Institute of Bangladesh’s Employee of the Year in 2025 and is a member of the Bangladesh–China Joint Research Team on Export Capacity Enhancement.
